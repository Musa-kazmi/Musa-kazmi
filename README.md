### 🚀 Featured Projects

**🧵 Textile Fabric Defect Detection** *(Capstone — in progress)*
Two-part QC system for Pakistan's textile export sector: a YOLOv8s detector ("the Eye") trained on the Chenab Textile dataset, achieving **mAP50 = 0.846**, exceeding the published benchmark, paired with a GenAI reporting layer ("the Writer") that auto-generates compliance reports aligned with the EU Digital Product Passport requirements.

**🐟 AquaVision — Aquarium Fish Species Classifier**
CNN-based fish species classifier deployed with Streamlit.
[`Aquarium-Fish-Classifier`](https://github.com/Musa-kazmi/Aquarium-Fish-Classifier)

**📈 PSX Stock Prediction Pipeline**
Compared 7 model families for KSE-100 direction prediction, with an honest, data-driven conclusion on the realistic accuracy ceiling for price-history-only forecasting — built with a Streamlit comparison app.

**🌾 Crop Disease Detector**
CV-based disease detection with a WhatsApp + Urdu voice interface, making diagnostics accessible to non-technical farmers.

**🔍 UET RAG Agent**
Dual-path retrieval system combining semantic search (ChromaDB + local sentence-transformers) and text-to-SQL (SQLite) with Gemini for generation.

**📰 Fake News Classifier**
TF-IDF + Logistic Regression classifier, **F1 = 0.903**.
